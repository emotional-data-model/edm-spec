**Draft R18 — the coverage clause.** Plan §7 says a cell "with coverage below 50 % of texts (exclusions plus nulls) is uncovered and counts as not held". Read literally (the reading this run applies), a null-valued answer reduces coverage, so a stated-only field that is null by design on most texts cannot be held by any model, however well the models agree on when it is null and what it says when populated: `turning_point` (null 0.89 in the majority), `self_position` (0.79), `bodily_sensation` (0.79), `attribution` (0.78), `function` (0.73) are uncovered for every arm, and `harm` (0.33) and `meaning` (0.54) for most. Run 1 earned these fields their comparable class with null as a category of the κ, never as missing coverage. The founder should rule which reading the clause carries: (a) literal, in which case ten fields re-class interpretive and Essential falls to 9; or (b) "nulls" = invalid outputs, in which case the sensitivity column decides and the drafts below read accordingly (two fields re-class: `tether`, and `summary` only until its judge pass is complete). The pre-registered text is (a); the pre-registration's own evidence base is (b). Recommendation: rule (b) prospectively and record (a)'s outcome beside it, since the clause was written to catch arms that answer rarely, not fields that are rightly null.
