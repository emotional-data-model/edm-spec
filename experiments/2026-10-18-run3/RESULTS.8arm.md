## 2. Per-field verdict (5-of-9 rule) with the framing bound

"Comparable holds across the market" when ≥ 5 of the 9 models hold the field (the three voters + ≥ 2 of the 6 new arms). **Coverage rule, read literally as frozen:** a cell is *uncovered* when fewer than 50 % of texts have both a majority and a non-null answer from the arm, so a stated-only field that is null by design on most texts (`turning_point`, `attribution`, `function`, `self_position`, `bodily_sensation`) is uncovered for every arm and fails by construction. The frozen verdict is reported as scored; the **sensitivity column** re-reads "nulls" as *invalid outputs* (a null-valued answer counts as covered, which is how run 1 scored these fields, with null as a category). **R18 (recorded 2026-10-18 after the run, `rulings-2026-10-18.md`; deviation R3-D4 in §0.1) adopts the sensitivity reading, (b), prospectively: it decides the rulings R19–R28 and any later run; the literal reading, (a), is the frozen pre-registration and stays reported beside it.** The scoring code was not changed: the (b) column is the one computed before the ruling. Framing bound (Q5) = Sonnet 5.5 under the 47-field run-1 prompt (7-field run-2 Arm B for `harm`) against Sonnet 5.5 under this 19-field prompt: Cohen κ (raw agreement) for coded / boolean, ICC for `intensity.value`, judge same-class rate (populated-only beside) for free text. A new arm whose agreement is at or above the bound cannot be said to disagree for vendor reasons. Pre-registered recommendation on failure: re-class `interpretive` (R17: Essential → Extended); the founder rules.
