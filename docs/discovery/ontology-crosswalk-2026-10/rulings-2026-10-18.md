## R3 — Run 1 cannot separate field reliability from prompt description quality

**Ruling under delegation, veto by exception.** Run 1 tested the six slots under their v0.9 names, inside a 47-field prompt, with `echo` reshaped as a list; the v0.8.3 descriptions and examples were present for the other five. Run 1 therefore cannot tell whether the cross-model disagreement comes from the slot definitions, the framing, or the texts admitting several valid fillers; the diagnostic of 2026-10-18 (experiments/2026-10-18-diagnostic/RESULTS.md: 42% multiple valid referents, 20% granularity, 24% null-vs-populated, 13% extractor error) separates those, and run 2 tests selection and null rules on Arm B.

Figures that make the confound visible: the four short slots are repeatable in Sonnet 5.5 (0.80–0.87) and in no other extractor (0.55–0.76), and the extractors name different referents (cross 0.38–0.54; populated-only 0.41–0.56). A definition that one model reads one way and three models read other ways is the signature of an under-specified prompt as much as of an unreliable field; run 1 cannot tell them apart.

*Accuracy note (delegated session, 2026-10-18).* Checked against `prompts/extraction_system.txt`: for the five renamed slots (`subject`, `trigger`, `harm`, `drive`, `integration`) the run-1 prompt did reproduce the v0.8.3 `description` and the `1–5 words (e.g., …)` constraint line verbatim under the new names (`draft_schema_build.py` copies them from `fields.json`). What was not carried: the v0.8.3 field names (`anchor`, `spark`, `wound`, `fuel`, `bridge`, `echo`), the seven-field `core` framing (each slot sat among 47 fields with v0.9 rules), and for `echo` everything — its description ("What continues to resonate or recur from the experience"), its constraint and its examples were replaced by the merged `retrieval_cues` description and the list shape. The ruling's conclusion stands as written (one prompt; the six slots were not tested as the v0.8.3 text); the sentence "descriptions, 1–5 word constraints and examples were not carried" is exact for `echo` and for the slot set as named and framed, and over-states the case for the other five taken individually. Recorded so the founder can veto or re-word; run 2's Arm A is the v0.8.3 text verbatim either way.

Applied: `experiment-plan-run2.md`; REPORT.md §7.1 and `v0.9-proposal.md` §10 state R3.

## R8 — Salience is carried as a per-extractor numeric weight

**Ruling under delegation, veto by exception.** The salience signal EDM carries is the per-extractor numeric weight: `emotion.intensity.value` and `salience.linkage_strength` (persistence rank contributed nothing: `S_persistence` pooled 1 of 4). Task (a) passed pooled, synthetic and Pepys. The conversational arm test was uninformative by construction and is redesigned in run 2.

| Arm (`S_all`, k = 3) | Δ over recency: Sonnet / Haiku / GPT / Kimi | extractors ≥ +0.10 | recency mean | random floor | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pooled | +0.19 / +0.14 / +0.16 / +0.21 | 4 | 0.23 | 0.29 | pass |
| synthetic | +0.50 / +0.40 / +0.35 / +0.45 | 4 | 0.25 | 0.30 | pass |
| pepys | +0.10 / 0.00 / +0.10 / +0.15 | 3 | 0.25 | 0.30 | pass |
| conversational | −0.03 / +0.02 / +0.02 / +0.03 | 0 | 0.19 | 0.28 | fail, uninformative |

Components pooled: `S_intensity` 3 of 4, `S_linkage` 3 of 4, `S_persistence` 1 of 4. Why the conversational arm is uninformative by construction (RESULTS.md §0.2): LoCoMo probes are spread across sessions so any k-of-n retention policy scores near k / n, and both policies sit below the random floor; LongMemEval blocks have two answer sessions among eight seeded distractors, so hit rates move in steps of 0.5 over three blocks. Run 2 rebuilds the LoCoMo ground truth on the sessions the QA set cites as evidence (`experiment-plan-run2.md` §6).

"Per-extractor" is a property of the data: `gpt-6.1-sol` leaves `intensity.value` null 93 % and `linkage_strength` null 90 % of the time while the Claude models fill them; the weight is comparable within an extractor, and that is the comparability EDM claims for it.

Applied: `experiment-plan-run2.md` §6; `v0.9-proposal.md` §10.
