## 0. What run 3 is

Runs 1 and 2 classed every v0.9 field by how three voting extractors (Claude Sonnet 5.5, gpt-6.1-sol, Kimi K2.6) behave on 150 texts, and R17 made the profiles follow that class: the 19 fields classed **comparable** are the Essential profile. "Comparable" so far means *comparable across those three vendors*. Run 3 asks whether it holds across the market: the same 150 texts and the same thresholds, the 19 comparable-tier fields only, six further models from six further vendor lines pinned through one gateway (OpenRouter), five repeats each. No new field, no new text, no new threshold.

Two further arms run under the same prompt and are reported beside the six, never counted among the 9: **Claude Haiku 5.5** as the cheap-tier reference, and **Claude Sonnet 5.5 re-run under the 19-field prompt**, whose answers supply the Sonnet component of the majority that Q2 compares against (the gpt-6.1-sol and Kimi K2.6 components stay from run 1, or run 2 Arm B for `harm`), and whose agreement with its own run-1 (47-field) answers is reported as the **framing bound** on §4's limitation.

What run 3 decides (§7): per model × field, whether the model repeats the field and agrees with the voting majority; per field, whether "comparable" holds across ≥ 5 of 9 models in total (the three voters plus the six new arms). What it does not decide: profile membership directly (R17 derives it from class; §7 states the pre-registered recommendation if a field fails, and the founder rules).

Two consequences, pre-registered: (i) the Anthropic prompt cache is requested through `cache_control` on the system text part, which OpenRouter forwards to the Anthropic endpoint; cache reads and writes are read back from the gateway's usage and the billed cost is used; (ii) runs 1–2 called the judge with `output_config.effort = "low"` and no `thinking` block (measured output 322–344 tokens per call, that is, no extended thinking). **Gateway rules (fixed now).** Base URL `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1`, key `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` (present in `.env`); OpenAI-compatible chat completions through the run-1 `Provider` class with a new provider kind `openrouter`, `response_format: json_object` as on the OpenAI-compatible arms of runs 1–2, `max_tokens` 12,000. The bound is reported beside every Q2 figure and does not change a verdict. The Haiku reference arm gives a second such bound for one cheap-tier model.

## 5. Judge (free-text fields only; one pass covers repeatability, agreement with the majority and the framing bound)

For each text and each free-text field the judge receives **all** run-3 answers for that field (8 arms × 5 repeats = 40, indexed, arm hidden) **plus the two other voters' representatives** from the run that classed the field (gpt-6.1-sol and Kimi K2.6; run 1 for `summary`, `meaning`, `self_position`, `bodily_sensation`; run 2 Arm B for `harm`; representative = the answer from the voter's largest within-model equivalence class in that run's judge grouping, re-read from `raw/judge/` of that run, ties → earliest repeat) **plus Sonnet 5.5's representative from that same run** (for the framing bound only), 43 answers in all, and groups them into equivalence classes under rules 1–5 (`experiments/2026-10-18-run2/prompts/judge_system.txt`, verbatim; rule 5: length is not a difference, judge the referent); the judge runs without extended thinking through the gateway (§3 note). Null answers form the "null" class and are not sent. From one grouping the scorer reads, per arm: repeatability (fraction of its repeat pairs in the same class), the **majority class** (the class holding ≥ 2 of the three voter representatives: the Sonnet re-run's representative from this run, the gpt-6.1-sol and Kimi representatives from the classing run; a text where no two share a class is excluded for that field and the exclusion rate is reported), **majority agreement** (the arm's representative falls in the majority class) and the **framing bound** (Sonnet's run-3 representative and its classing-run representative fall in the same class).

Judge calls: per text, `summary` alone and the other four free-text fields together = **2 calls per text, 300 calls**, up to 43 answers per field.

## 6. Scoring without a judge (coded, boolean, numeric)

Per arm and field, over the 150 texts, exactly as `experiment-plan.md` §4.2 with the voters' majority as the comparator:

- **Repeatability:** Fleiss κ over subjects = texts, raters = 5 repeats, categories = normalised labels (lower-cased, trimmed, deprecated values mapped per `DEPRECATED_VALUES`, null its own category); `intensity.value` ICC(2,1) over texts × repeats on texts where all five repeats give a number (coverage reported).
- **Majority label per text:** each voter's modal label over its five repeats (ties → earliest repeat, as run 1) — Sonnet 5.5 from **this run**, gpt-6.1-sol and Kimi K2.6 from run 1; the majority = the label ≥ 2 of 3 voters share; texts with no majority are excluded for that field (exclusion rate reported). For `intensity.value`: the voters' per-text mean over the voters that gave a number.
- **Majority agreement:** Cohen's κ between the arm's modal label and the majority label over the included texts; for `intensity.value`, ICC(2,1) between the arm's per-text mean and the voters' per-text mean over texts where both exist. For the Sonnet re-run itself the figure is reported but is bounded by construction (it is a component of the majority); it is read only as a check.
- **Framing bound (Q5):** Cohen's κ between Sonnet's run-1 modal label and its run-3 modal label per field (raw agreement beside it); ICC(2,1) for `intensity.value`.
- Also reported: null rate per arm × field; non-canonical rate; cross-model κ among the six new arms (descriptive); invalid-output rate per arm; the `provider` field distribution per arm; reasoning-token share per gateway arm.

## 7. Decision rule (fixed now)

Thresholds verbatim from run 1 (`experiment-plan.md` §4.1): repeatability κ ≥ 0.70 (coded / boolean), judge-equivalence ≥ 0.80 (free text), ICC ≥ 0.75 (value); agreement κ ≥ 0.40 (coded / boolean), judge-equivalence ≥ 0.60 (free text), ICC ≥ 0.50 (value), here applied to **agreement with the majority** rather than to cross-model agreement among the arms. Borderline (within 0.05) and sparse (null ≥ 90 %) flags as in run 1; a field × arm cell with coverage below 50 % of texts (exclusions plus nulls) is reported as **uncovered** and counts as not held.

- **Per model × field:** the arm **holds** the field when both thresholds are met; **repeats only** when repeatability is met and agreement is not; **does not hold** otherwise. This is the model × field capability table.
- **Per field:** "comparable" **holds across the market** when ≥ 5 of the 9 models hold it, the three voters counting as holding by the run that classed the field (so the rule needs ≥ 2 of the 6 new arms). Reported beside it, descriptively and not deciding: the count among the six new arms alone, and the framing bound.
- **Pre-registered recommendation if a field fails the 5-of-9 rule:** re-class it `interpretive` (repeatable within some extractors, not comparable across the market), which under R17 moves it from Essential to Extended. Recommendation only; the founder rules, veto by exception.
- **Reference and re-run arms:** Haiku 5.5 and the Sonnet 5.5 re-run are scored identically and reported in the same table but are not among the 9 and never change a field verdict.

**Pre-registered predictions** (fixed with this draft; RESULTS.md reports each as held / not held; they do not alter the decision rule). Baseline figures from runs 1–2 on these 19 fields (lead units; repeatability met under the frozen thresholds): Sonnet 5.5 19 of 19, Kimi K2.6 19 of 19, gpt-6.1-sol 18 of 19 (misses `intensity.value`, which it leaves null 93 % of the time), **Haiku 5.5 11 of 19** (misses `harm` 0.78, `summary` 0.79, `scope` 0.69, `function` 0.68, `action_tendency` 0.66, `need` 0.68, `temporal_focus` 0.61, `coping` 0.69). **Budget: USD 50, hard stop.** Pilot gate: after the 24 pilot calls, project per arm = measured per-call cost × 750 and total = Σ arms + judge reserve 9; if total > 50 apply **in this order** (pre-registered now):

1. **Reasoning effort low first.** On each arm that exposes a reasoning parameter and whose measured per-call cost exceeds 1.5 × its projected line above, set `reasoning: {effort: "low"}` (OpenRouter) and log it as a deviation for that arm; re-pilot that arm (3 calls) and re-project.
2. **Raise the stop, keep every arm.** If the projection still exceeds 50, raise the hard stop to **USD 60** and keep all six arms, the reference and the re-run; log it.
3. **Drop only above 60, and never Gemini.** If the projection exceeds 60, drop the **costliest open-weight arm by projection** (among N2, N3, N4, N6 — N1 Gemini is never dropped; N5 is not open-weight at freeze and is not in the drop set); the 5-of-9 rule then reads 5-of-8, never relaxed; repeat until the projection is ≤ 60.
4. Do not start.

Re-project after every 1,000 calls against the stop in force; the judge pass runs only if spent + 9 ≤ the stop in force, re-projects after its first 10 calls and stops if its own projection exceeds the remaining budget.

**Credit ceiling (stated at freeze).** The OpenRouter account holds USD 50.00 of credit (0 used; key limit 100), and every call of this run goes through it (§3). The balance, not the stop, is what this session can spend: lever 2 keeps every arm as the founder instructed, and if the credits run out before the run completes, the run stops checkpointed with the unfinished calls listed in STATUS.md for the founder to top up and resume; no arm is dropped for lack of credit.
