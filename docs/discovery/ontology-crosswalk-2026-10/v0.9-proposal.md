## 2. Opt-in trait profile

`theoretical-conflicts.md` §5 separates event-level description from person-level trait. Two v0.8.3 fields are read as traits by every framework that names them: `attachment_style` (Ainsworth / Main; Bartholomew) and `regulation_state` (Gross's model names strategies of a person, not states of an event). Proposal:

- A `trait` block exists in the schema but belongs to **no canonical profile**. Essential, Extended and Full omit it. A partner profile opts in by listing `trait.attachment_style` and/or `trait.regulation_state` in `included_fields` (the §3.7.6 mechanism; the therapy profile is the obvious candidate).
- Both fields keep their v0.8.3 enums as `text` and gain codings: attachment on EDM vocab `attachment_style/v1` with Ainsworth / Main letter codes as display (A avoidant, B secure, C anxious, D disorganized; Bartholomew prototypes `related`); regulation on EDM vocab `regulation_state/v1` (Gross names no state list).
- The field description reads "as stated by the subject for this experience". The artifact never aggregates trait fields across artifacts; a consumer that wants a person-level classification does so downstream and outside the spec, which is what `SCOPE_AND_NONGOALS.md` already says about attachment.
- Migration: a v0.8.3 artifact carrying either field keeps a `trait` block only when the target profile opts in (the draft validator composes `base + trait` for partner profiles that list `trait.*`); otherwise the migration tool moves the values verbatim to `extensions["org.emotionaldatamodel.migration"].out_of_profile_fields`. Text is preserved on both paths; see `draft-schema/README.md` for the validation runs.

## 3. `narrative.self_position` and `narrative.meaning` after run 1 (rulings R7 and R6, 2026-10-18)

Both rulings are *ruling under delegation, veto by exception* (`rulings-2026-10-18.md`); figures from `experiments/2026-10-17/RESULTS.md`.

### 3.1 `narrative.self_position` — stays, stated-only (R7)

**Definition (unchanged).** The subject's own expressed lens on themselves within the story: the position they say they occupy ("the one who holds it together", "the kid who got left behind", "the person who finally said no"). Free text. Stated-only: recorded only when the subject says it or paraphrases it unmistakably; never assigned from the plot, the emotion, or an archetype catalogue.

**Rationale (R7).** The field is **a record of how the person positions themselves in the story**. That is the whole claim. The earlier rationale — that a stated self-position changes how the rest of the record is read — is **withdrawn**: task (b) restored the field to a blanked artifact and moved 12 % of rater readings of `arc` / `trajectory` / `need` (n = 720), against 9 % for a neutral control field (`context.place`), under a frozen threshold of ≥ 20 % with control < 10 %. The field carries information; it does not demonstrably change interpretation, and the proposal no longer says it does.

**Why it still replaces `narrative_archetype`.** The 12-value archetype enum is the Mark & Pearson (2001) brand set [KNOWLEDGE]; six of its values map to nothing in 39 frameworks and the field as a whole is weakly grounded (`REPORT.md` §4). It also asks the extractor to classify a person, which the interpretation-only constraint forbids. A stated self-position is the nearest construct the frameworks actually have, and run 1 shows it is the most repeatable free-text unit in the layout: repeatability 0.90–0.96 across the four extractors, cross-model 0.87 (0.81 on populated answers), null 79 %, pass pooled and in every arm. Null precision on the 30 synthetic texts with no stated self-position was 0.87 / 0.63 / 0.67 / 0.93 (Sonnet / Haiku / GPT / Kimi), so the stated-only wording leaks for the weaker extractors; the wording is kept as drafted and the leak is recorded (§10), not corrected by this proposal.

**Codings (all optional, additive).**

| System | Code set | Relation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| McAdams imagoes | open list; the coder writes the imago label the subject's words evoke (e.g. "the caregiver", "the survivor") | `related` | McAdams 1985, 1993: idealised personified images of the self as main characters in the life story (`frameworks.json#mcadams`, definition confirmed in this verification pass) |
| McAdams agency / communion | `agency`, `communion`, with Foley Center sub-themes as narrower codes (self-mastery, status/victory, achievement/responsibility, empowerment; love/friendship, dialogue, caring/help, unity/togetherness) | `related` | McAdams & McLean 2013; Foley Center coding manual (`frameworks.json#mcadams`) |
| Hermans I-positions | free label of the "I-position" voiced (e.g. "I as protector") | `related` | Hermans, H. J. M., Kempen, H. J. G. & van Loon, R. J. P. (1992). The dialogical self: beyond individualism and rationalism. *American Psychologist* 47(1), 23–33; Hermans (2001) *Culture & Psychology* 7(3), 243–281 [KNOWLEDGE: not in the reference set; cite for the construct that a self is a multiplicity of positions the person can voice] |

Not the Mark & Pearson 12. If a partner wants the archetype set it lives in `extensions`, exactly as `naming-proposal.md` row 18 proposed, and never feeds `self_position`.

**Experiment outcome (`REPORT.md` §7 rank 12; task (b) of `experiment-plan.md` §6).** The rater study was run on 20 synthetic texts with a stated self-position, four rater models × 3 repeats, conditions base / + `self_position` / + control field: shift 0.12 vs 0.09 → **fail** under the frozen threshold. Per field: `arc` 0.11 vs 0.09, `trajectory` 0.17 vs 0.12, `need` 0.06 vs 0.05; `valence_shift` (descriptive) 0.45 vs 0.49. The secondary question (how often a self-position is actually stated per genre) was answered descriptively: null 47 % on the synthetic arm, 95 % conversational, 96 % Pepys.

### 3.2 `narrative.meaning` — interpretive, attributed; not stated-only (R6)

**What run 1 showed.** The draft defined `meaning` with the v0.8.3 text ("The explicit realization or conclusion stated by the subject"; constraint "Short sentence. Extracted, not inferred") and the extraction prompt listed it as STATED-ONLY. On the 25 synthetic texts authored with no stated meaning, every extractor wrote one anyway: Sonnet 5.5 on 62 %, Haiku 5.5 on 82 %, gpt-6.1-sol on 94 %, Kimi K2.6 on 74 %. On the 25 texts with a stated meaning all four populated it 100 %. Agreement is high regardless: repeatability 0.83–0.93, cross-model 0.71, 0.67 on populated answers; pass pooled and in all three arms.

**Ruling (R6).** `narrative.meaning` is reclassified as **interpretive, attributed**: an interpretation of what the text states, attributable to a passage, in the same sense as `emotion.primary` or `narrative.summary` — not a quotation-only field, and no longer in the stated-only list. The field as written invites a one-sentence reading of the point of the experience, and that is what every extractor reliably produces; calling it stated-only claimed a null precision the run did not show (0.06–0.38 against a 0.95 target).

**Draft text (applied to `draft-schema/fragments/narrative.json` via `scripts/v09_layout.py` `DESC`).** Description: "The realization or conclusion the subject draws from the experience, as the text supports it. Interpretive, attributed: an interpretation attributable to a passage, not a quotation; null only when the text offers no basis." Constraint: "Short sentence. Interpretive, attributed (R6 2026-10-18); v0.8.3 read 'Extracted, not inferred'." The v0.8.3 wording is preserved verbatim in the crosswalk record (`v083.description` / `v083.constraints`). Any future prompt drops `meaning` from the stated-only list. The stated-only fields that *did* hold null precision (`emotion.bodily_sensation` 0.98–1.00; `emotion.appraisal.attribution` 0.65–1.00; `narrative.turning_point` 0.95–1.00) keep the stated-only label.

## 4. Coding shape, value words, dimensions

The pattern is `coding-proposal.md`'s: FHIR R4 `Coding` (`system`, `version`, `code`, `display`) plus an EDM `relation` (`exact` default | `broader` | `narrower` | `related`).

```json
"coping": {
  "text": "reframe_meaning",
  "codings": [
    {"system": "https://emotionaldatamodel.org/vocab/coping/v1", "version": "1", "code": "reframe_meaning", "display": "reframe_meaning", "relation": "exact"},
    {"system": "https://emotionaldatamodel.org/xref/v1/lazarus", "code": "positive_reappraisal", "display": "Positive Reappraisal", "relation": "exact"},
    {"system": "https://emotionaldatamodel.org/xref/v1/gross", "code": "cognitive_change", "display": "cognitive change", "relation": "exact"}
  ]
}
```

Rules:

1. `text` is the interpretation and is mandatory when the field is populated. Codings are optional and additive; they may disagree with each other; a coding never replaces or rewrites the text.
2. **Value words are unchanged.** `wonder`, `peace`, `tenderness`, `longing`, `belonging`, `mastery`, `explore`, `share`, `confront`, `protect`, `detach`, `reframe_meaning`, `seek_support`, `formative_experience`, `milestone` all stay exactly as they are; the Frijda, Ways of Coping, SDT, Pillemer and MFOEM terms are applied as codes (see the layout tables). The value-rename table in `naming-proposal.md` is withdrawn in favour of this rule.
3. Every canonical value gets an EDM code (`https://emotionaldatamodel.org/vocab/<field>/v1#<value>`), so an artifact's `text` is also its EDM code and the canonical list becomes a *recommended* vocabulary rather than an enum. Free text in a canonical-open field gets no EDM code.
4. Deprecated values (10, listed in the crosswalk record) remain valid `text`; the vocabulary marks them `owl:deprecated` and, where one exists, points at the canonical replacement (friend → friendship, couple → romantic_partnership, fluid → transient, looping / cyclic → recurring). Nothing is renamed in any artifact.
5. **Dimensions.** `intensity` carries `value` (the existing 0..1 `emotional_weight`) beside an optional ordinal `text`; `valence`, `urgency` and `control` carry only the ordinal `text` plus codings, and `arousal` is born as text plus codings (R5, applied 2026-10-18: the four `value` members were never populated by any extractor in run 1 and are deleted from the draft). The migration **never derives a number from a label**. The dimensional scales (EmotionML `fsre-dimensions#valence`, `pad-dimensions#arousal`, `ema-appraisals#urgency`, `scherer-appraisals#control`) remain available as codings, so a dimensional reading is a later addition, not a reversal.

## 5. The six new fields and `telemetry.field_confidence`

All six are **stated-only** in the same sense as every EDM field: populated only when the source expresses the content, never inferred from other fields.

| Field | Definition | Values / coding |
|---|---|---|
| `emotion.kind` | Whether the subject describes the affect as an episode with an object, a diffuse state without one, a felt bodily quality, or a standing disposition. Default `emotion` when the subject does not distinguish. | `emotion` → MFOEM:000001; `mood` → MFOEM:000193; `feeling` → MFOEM:000006; `disposition` → MFOEM:000199. Damasio's emotion / feeling split as `related`. |
| `emotion.arousal` | The stated activation level of the experience (keyed up vs calm), independent of valence and of intensity. | text low / medium / high; codings on EmotionML `pad-dimensions#arousal`, `fsre-dimensions#arousal`, NRC VAD arousal (no numeric `value`, R5). Russell's second axis. |
| `emotion.appraisal.attribution` | To whom or what the subject attributes the cause of the event ("I blamed myself", "it was just bad luck"). | `self` → MFOEM:000076 / EmotionML `agent-self`; `other` → MFOEM:000078 / `agent-other`; `chance` → MFOEM:000077 / `agent-nature`; Lazarus blame / credit as `related`. Explicit content only. |
| `context.event_category` | Coded companion to free-text `context.event`. | Singer & Blagov / Thorne & McLean: relationships, life_threatening_events, recreation_exploration, achievement_mastery, guilt_shame, drug_alcohol_tobacco_use, not_classifiable (labels knowledge-reconstructed; see `REPORT.md` §9). |
| `context.relationship_model` | Coded companion to `context.relationship`: the relational model the subject's account of the relationship instantiates. | Fiske: communal_sharing, authority_ranking, equality_matching, market_pricing, null (fetched). |
| `salience.function` | Why the subject says the memory matters: to guide action, to define the self, or to connect with others. Multi-valued. | Pillemer 1992 / Bluck 2003: `directive`, `self`, `social` (`frameworks.json#pillemer` memory_functions). |

`telemetry.field_confidence` is an infrastructure field, not an affective one: an object keyed by field path (`"emotion.primary": 0.92`) carrying the extractor's confidence per populated field, after EmotionML's `confidence` attribute, which every EmotionML annotation may carry. It sits beside `entry_confidence` (whole-artifact). It is excluded from the seal hash like the rest of telemetry.

**Anthropic 171 — telemetry cross-reference only.** The 171 emotion words from "Emotion concepts and their function in a large language model" (2026; `sources/anthropic_171.json`, count verified) describe functional concepts inside a Claude model, not a theory of human emotion (`theoretical-conflicts.md` §10). They are not a vocabulary for `emotion.primary` and do not appear in the affective core or in `vocab/`. Their only proposed use is an audit hook: an extraction run on a Claude model may record in `telemetry.extraction_notes` (or a `crosswalks` entry) whether the emotion written into `emotion.primary` corresponds to one of the 171 concepts, as a consistency check on the extractor, never as a code on the field.

## 6. Vocabulary namespace stub

`vocab/edm-vocab-v1.ttl` is the first concrete artefact of the namespace `https://emotionaldatamodel.org/vocab/` (proposed; does not resolve). It covers the seven vocabularies asked for and is generated from `crosswalk.csv`, so it inherits the crosswalk's relation judgements and verification flags:

| Measure | Count |
|---|---|
| Concept schemes | 7 (arc 20 concepts, tether 9, relationship 19, memory_type 5, temporal_profile 10, need 6, coping 7) |
| EDM concepts | 76 |
| `skos:*Match` triples | 254 (36 exactMatch, 218 relatedMatch; the crosswalk has no broader/narrower cells for these fields) |
| Placeholder target concepts | 143 under `https://emotionaldatamodel.org/xref/v1/<framework>#<slug>` |
| Real target URIs | MFOEM classes as OBO PURLs |

The placeholder mechanism is the honest part: Booker, Campbell, McAdams, Lazarus, Pillemer, SDT, Fiske and the rest publish no concept URIs, and the EmotionML vocabularies identify items by name inside a vocabulary URI. Each placeholder carries the framework citation URL and the V / K / - flag so a reader can see whether the target label was ever verified against a fetched list. Conventions and the regeneration command are in `vocab/README.md`.

It is generated by `scripts/v09_layout.py`, so the record and the migration implementation cannot drift.

**Artifact text is unchanged.** The same script migrates `synthetic-examples/example-full-profile.json` (the published example describes a real person, so the discovery folder uses a synthetic replacement with the same structure) and compares the multiset of every scalar (strings, numbers, booleans) in the five v0.8.3 affective domains with the multiset in the five v0.9 domains plus `trait` plus the legacy extension, with `codings` stripped. What the migration does and does not do: it renames paths, wraps categorical values, applies deterministic codes from the crosswalk, concatenates the three retrieval-cue fields, collects both arc fields into `arc[]`, carries the numeric intensity, and copies cut-field values into the legacy extension. It leaves every new field null, every non-intensity `value` null, and `self_position` null (an archetype label is not a stated self-position). Reverse migration is the field map inverted and is lossy only for codings and new fields.

### 10.6 Run 3 and the rulings applied (R18–R30, 2026-10-18 close)

Source: `experiments/2026-10-18-run3/RESULTS.md` (5,250 extraction calls: 150 texts × 7 arms × 5 repeats, every arm through OpenRouter pinned to one hosting endpoint; judge Opus 5.5 with rule 5, 194 of 300 calls, incomplete on credit; USD 49.35 of a 50 credit; pre-registration frozen at `974408e` before any call). Under (b) 17 of 19 hold.
